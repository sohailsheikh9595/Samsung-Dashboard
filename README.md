Samsung Supply Chain & Logistics Dashboard
📊 Overview

The Samsung Supply Chain & Logistics Dashboard is an interactive business intelligence dashboard designed to monitor and analyze key performance indicators (KPIs) across the supply chain, manufacturing, inventory, shipment, and sales operations.

The dashboard provides a consolidated view of business performance through interactive visualizations, KPI cards, and comparative charts. It helps stakeholders understand supply chain efficiency, track inventory levels, evaluate shipment performance, analyze supplier lead times, and monitor revenue and procurement costs.

✨ Key Features
Executive Overview: Displays essential business KPIs, including total revenue, profit, profit margin, cost ratio, procurement cost, cash cycle days, and discount rate.
Supplier Analysis: Visualizes supplier lead times to help identify supplier performance and delivery efficiency.
Manufacturing Insights: Provides an overview of manufacturing operations and production-related metrics.
Inventory Management: Tracks current stock levels, safety stock, and product-wise inventory distribution.
Shipment Tracking: Monitors total quantity shipped, delivered quantities, shipment distribution, and shipment costs.
Sales Analytics: Analyzes revenue contribution across different customer segments and retail channels.
KPI Monitoring: Presents key metrics using summary cards, bar charts, stacked bar charts, and doughnut charts.
Executive-Friendly Interface: Uses a clean, organized layout to make important business insights easy to understand.
